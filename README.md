# Office Disgrace

*A roguelite work simulator. It's Monday. Again.*

You wake up at 6:47 AM, go to work, and face your performance review at 5:00 PM. Then you wake up at 6:47 AM, and it's the same Monday.

Each loop resets your day but not what you know. Learn when things happen, use it on the next loop, and find a way to reach Tuesday.

## How to play

Open `index.html` in any browser, phone or desktop. Progress saves in the browser.

- Pick **Public Sector** (City of Bramblewood) or **Private Sector** (Veltrix Solutions).
- Manage three meters: **Output**, **Standing** and **Sanity**.
- Scheduled events happen at the same time every loop. A couple of random ones shuffle in each day.
- Your **Journal** keeps what you learn. **Habits** build up through repetition and make you faster.
- Passing the review isn't enough. There are four ways out of Monday.
